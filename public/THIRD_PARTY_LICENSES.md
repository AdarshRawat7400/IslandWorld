**Revolver Game Asset** by loafbrr_1 (source archive copyright field `loafbrr`) is released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) on [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/revolver-game-asset). The [combat source record](assets/combat/SOURCES.md) describes the source archive, conversion, and SHA-256 hashes. Attribution is voluntary under CC0. Their procedural walnut, steel, fabric, and skin maps are made at runtime. These project-created assets require no third-party media notice.
