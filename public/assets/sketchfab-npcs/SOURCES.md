## Old Fisherman

- **Runtime file:** `public/assets/sketchfab-npcs/old-fisherman-bente-schoone-optimized.glb`

- **Creator:** Bente Schoone
- **Model:** [Old Fisherman](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/old-fisherman-1359f1408b6c4dccae835f26cc70f5f2)
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit the creator, model, source, and license wherever the asset is distributed with the game; note modifications if made.
- **Official download:** Sketchfab's `1k texture` glTF binary (`.glb`) export, downloaded on 2026-09-29. The source-only file is unmodified; only its local filename was changed.
- **Original SHA-256:** `BC6FE84BF5DDDF055EAB965BCC4B548AA1883E5D204F1325CD307DECB755A058` (13,456,364 bytes; 12.83 MiB).
- **Optimized SHA-256:** `F5675D7B4CEBD0D6A4605974983220FB1B368A968614F819372FF432CA33C184` (13,201,760 bytes; 12.59 MiB).
- **Contents:** 28,754 triangles in 96 meshes, six materials and 14 embedded 1024 × 1024 PNG textures. This is a static seated-fisherman vignette with a hat, chair, fishing rod and line, buckets, fish, and decorative ground/water pieces. There is no boat, skeleton, or animation.

The runtime copy was made in Blender 5.1.2: all original mesh transforms were baked into geometry, then 96 meshes and their 260-node hierarchy were joined into six meshes by shared material. The re-export retains every triangle, all six materials, all 14 textures, and the same world bounds to float-point precision. Decoded texture pixels are identical; tangents were generated by Blender during export. No decorative ground, water, chair, rod, fish, or other parts were deleted. This reduces the scene to approximately six draw calls. Transparent parts share two of those materials, so check their layering in the target browser renderer.

Island World's runtime hides the two joined decorative ground/water card batches, named `Material_1018` and `Material_1021` in the optimized GLB, because their bright flat surfaces clash with the island shore. The original and optimized GLBs still contain those meshes. The seated figure, chair, fishing rod, buckets, and fish remain visible.

Both the source and optimized export use Y up. The complete scene spans about `0.184 × 0.142 × 0.274` raw world units. Scale the runtime root by about `10` to make the seated figure roughly human-sized and the scene footprint about `1.84 × 2.74` world units. Its decorative ground plane is at raw Y `-0.0997`; place the root about `0.997` world units above the terrain at scale `10` to align that plane to the surface. The lowest decorative water pieces then sit about `0.21` units below the surface. These are placement recommendations, not changes to either stored asset.
