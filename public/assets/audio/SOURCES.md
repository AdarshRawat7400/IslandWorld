# Recorded ambience sources

The seven local recordings are renamed copies of files offered under **CC0** by their creators on OpenGameArt. Local SHA-256 hashes identify the bundled bytes. The game changes their playback volume and mix at runtime. Thunder, bird calls, engine tones, and other event sounds are synthesized by the game; they are not additional recordings.
