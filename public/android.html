Version: 1.0.0-beta.1 · Size: 146.4 MB

Requires: Android 10 or later, WebGL 2, and a recent Android System WebView.

The world assets are included in the app. Multiplayer and room voice require internet access. Microphone access is optional and requested only when you enable voice.

Install on your phone

  1. Download the APK from Google Drive. For a large file, Drive may ask you to confirm the download.
  2. Open the downloaded file and allow installation from that browser when Android asks.
  3. Install IslandWorld and play in landscape. Use the left stick to move, drag on the right to look, and tap SPRINT to run.

This is a directly installed beta app, not a Google Play listing. Install future releases over this app to keep your local settings. The app automatically uses the lighter mobile graphics profile; performance depends on your phone.
