# Sketchfab NPC model review

The [Sketchfab NPC search](https://sketchfab.com/search?q=tag%3Anpc&sort_by=-likeCount&type=models) contains many fantasy characters and models from existing games. Those do not fit Island World's grounded coastal setting or do not have clear reuse rights. Each candidate below was checked on its own model page on 29 September 2026. The user signed in to Sketchfab, so the two selected GLBs were downloaded through the official control. The island also uses the credited CC0 MakeHuman character assets.

| Model | Creator | Approx. triangles | Island fit | Status |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| [Gone fishing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gone-fishing-abdb06aa64e84462aa88eed6b25742f5) | Bree08 | 25,300 | Semi-realistic dockside fisherman | Included, CC BY 4.0 |
| [Old Fisherman](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/old-fisherman-1359f1408b6c4dccae835f26cc70f5f2) | Bente Schoone | 28,800 | Seated dockside elder; more stylized than the current cast | Included, CC BY 4.0 |
| [Male civilian v1](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/male-civilian-v1-866f91409a67408d848bca50d92f420c) | Michael Constantine | 40,800 | Island resident | Reviewed, not used |
| [Fisherman Fishing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fisherman-fishing-4ca43cc270fd42c78c9d0566dcb1a5f0) | volkanongun | 1,800 | Animated distant pier figure; visibly low poly | Reviewed, not used |

The two included assets were inspected, scaled for the scene, and attributed in `CREDITS.md`, `public/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`, and `public/assets/sketchfab-npcs/SOURCES.md`. The [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits commercial use with attribution. The model page's downloadable badge alone does not prove that a particular derivative is suitable for this game's renderer; the actual GLBs were checked before use.

Excluded: [Fisherman by wilsonghm99](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fisherman-5a2bbe8afc4e456399e3af6694fd5f54) is CC BY-NC, which would restrict commercial distribution. [Lighthouse Keeper by jeffeastin](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lighthouse-keeper-2e52963f895a427a8845021ae2ca3885) is over 700,000 triangles, too expensive for this realtime world without major retopology.
